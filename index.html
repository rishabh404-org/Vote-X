Cryptographic Safeguards

Zero-Knowledge Trust & Immutability

Vote-X separates voter identity from the cast ballot. Using client-side SHA-256 hashing, each ballot is anonymized, cryptographically sealed, and chained into a tamper-evident audit ledger.

lock

Ballot Secrecy Guarantee

Neither election officials nor network sniffers can link your vote back to your real identity.

verified

Verifiable Receipt Tokens

Every voter receives a unique cryptographic Transaction ID to audit that their ballot was tallied without revealing their choice.

block

Double-Voting Prevention

Single-use state locks ensure no voter can submit duplicate ballots under any circumstance.
